No Contact Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

The short version. Your breakup journey never leaves your phone. Every streak, mood, letter, red flag, and moment you save lives only on your device. We don't run a server that stores your content, and we can't read it even if we wanted to. Two narrow exceptions, both explained below: a small amount of anonymous usage and crash data (which you can turn off in Settings), and the AI coach, where the messages you choose to send are used only to generate the reply and are not stored by us.

1. Who we are

No Contact Tracker, also shown as NoContact in the app ("the app", "we", "us"), is a mobile application for iOS and Android, operated as an independent project by its developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information: a deliberately short list, because the app is designed to do as little as possible with it.

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of the rights described below, email us at nocontact.app.support@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

Everything you create inside the app is stored locally in your phone's private app storage. It is never uploaded to a server we run and never shared with any third party. This includes:

  • Your streak (start date, reset history)
  • Mood check-ins
  • Red flags you save
  • Unsent letters you write
  • Saved moments and time capsules
  • Your AI coach chat history (kept on your device only; see section 4)
  • Achievements you unlock
  • Your preferences (language, sounds, haptics, analytics toggle)

When you uninstall the app, this data is deleted by iOS or Android along with the app. We have no way to recover it. One note: your phone's own backup system (iCloud Backup on iOS, Google backup on Android) may include the app's local data, like it does for any app. That backup belongs to your Apple or Google account; we cannot access it.

4. The AI coach

The app includes an optional AI coach for the hard moments. It only does anything when you use it, and here is exactly what happens when you do:

  • The message you type, along with a small amount of context that makes the reply useful (such as your current day count and, where relevant, notes you have chosen to save in the app), is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our relay server and passed to OpenAI, which generates the reply.
  • We do not store your conversations. Our server processes the message in memory to generate the reply and keeps no copy. Your chat history exists only on your device.
  • The only thing our server keeps is an anonymous, per-device counter used to enforce fair daily usage limits. It contains no message content and cannot identify you.
  • OpenAI processes the text as a service provider under its API terms, which as of this writing do not permit using API data to train its models. Still, treat the coach like any online service: don't include names, addresses, or other identifying details you wouldn't want processed by a third party.

The coach offers supportive coaching, not therapy or medical care. In a crisis, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line.

5. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for, store, or transmit your name, email, phone number, or any account credential. The app has no login.
  • We do not use advertising or ad networks. The app does not show ads.
  • We do not track your location. The app never requests the location permission.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not sell data to anyone. There is no data for us to sell.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything about them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you want the pseudonymous analytics identifier associated with their device deleted, email us and we will help request deletion from the relevant services.

7. Your rights

Because your content lives only on your device, the easiest way to exercise most privacy rights is directly through your phone:

  • Access. Every piece of content you created is visible inside the app. There is no hidden profile we maintain about you.
  • Deletion. Uninstall the app. iOS or Android removes the local database; we never had a copy.
  • Analytics opt-out. Settings → anonymous analytics → off. This disables both Amplitude and Sentry.
  • Subscription cancellation. On iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Subscriptions. Governed by Apple's and Google's refund and cancellation policies.

If you are in the European Union, United Kingdom, California, or another jurisdiction that grants specific statutory privacy rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, etc.), you additionally have the right to request a copy of any data we hold that relates to you, to request correction, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Given how little we collect, our response will usually be: we hold nothing tied to you by name; here is the list of pseudonymous identifiers Amplitude, Sentry, and RevenueCat may still hold, and we will help you request deletion from those services. Email us and we will walk you through it.

8. Security

Your content is stored inside your phone's private per-app sandbox, which iOS and Android encrypt as part of device encryption. No other app on your phone can read it. All network traffic (to Amplitude, Sentry, RevenueCat, the coach relay, and the stores) uses HTTPS. We do not operate servers that store your content, so there is no content server for us to lose.

9. International data transfers

The providers listed above (Amplitude, Sentry, RevenueCat, OpenAI, Apple, Google) may process data on servers located in the United States or other countries. By using the app you acknowledge that anonymous telemetry, subscription receipts, and coach messages may be processed outside your country of residence, under those providers' own safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses, etc.).

10. Changes to this policy

If we materially change what the app collects or how, we will update this page and bump the "Last updated" date at the top. If the change is significant (for example, adding a new analytics provider or adding cloud sync for your content), we will also surface a one-time notice inside the app so you can review it before continuing.
