Questions, answered straight

Is No Contact Tracker free?

Free to download and free to start: the day counter, mood check-ins, and the core SOS tools cost nothing. A Pro subscription unlocks the full toolkit. No ads either way.

Is my journal private?

Yes. Your letters, red flags, moods, and coach chats are stored on your phone, not on our servers. We could not read them if we wanted to. The details are in our privacy policy.

What does the SOS button do?

It stands between you and the send button: guided breathing, the reasons you saved on a stronger day, and a short timer, because most urges pass within about ten minutes if you do not feed them.

What happens if I break my streak?

You reset the counter and keep going. The app keeps your history, and a slip after two clean weeks is still two weeks of healing. The goal is restarting fast, not never slipping.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Both. Same app, same tools, free to start on the App Store and Google Play.

Does the no contact rule actually work?

Distance is what lets your brain detach, and tracking makes the distance visible. We wrote a complete, honest guide on how and why it works.
